A printable bird-watching bingo card generator, built and donated to Grassroots Ecology for its community events.

Users drag and drop bird photos, and the app reads the common and Latin names from the filenames. A Fisher-Yates shuffle makes every card unique. Grids range from 3×3 to 6×6, and a print stylesheet lays out the cards for paper. Built with React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, and Radix UI.
