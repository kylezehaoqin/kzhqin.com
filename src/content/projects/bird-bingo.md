A donation project, shipped to Grassroots Ecology community events. It's a printable bird-watching bingo card generator.

Drag and drop the photos in, and the common and Latin names come from the filenames. A Fisher-Yates shuffle makes every card unique. Grids go from 3×3 to 6×6, and the CSS is built for the printer (Radix UI for the controls).
