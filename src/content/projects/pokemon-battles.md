A real-time multiplayer battle game modeled on Pokemon Showdown, built as a team project with Node.js, React, Firebase, and WebSockets.

My contributions:

- Developed and tested the shop API, including probabilistic "mystery egg" items drawn with stratified sampling over arbitrary weights, and its React UI.
- Implemented the battle service and the front-end battle state machine.
